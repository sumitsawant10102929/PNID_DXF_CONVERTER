Step 1 · Read

Every word on the sheet is read

OCR goes over the whole drawing and reads the equipment tags, line numbers, instrument tags and notes. The tags later give each valve, instrument and vessel its name.

Equipment tagsLine numbersInstrument tagsNotes
Step 2 · Detect

Symbols and off-page connectors are found

A detection model trained on the project legend finds the symbols (106 classes: valves, control valves, instruments, reducers and more), each with a confidence, and the off-page connectors.

106 symbol classesConfidence per symbolOPCs
Step 3 · Equipment

Equipment is outlined and named

Vessels, columns, tanks, pumps and exchangers are boxed and named by a vision-language model that runs on this server, so the drawing never leaves the machine.

OutlineTag & typeRuns on this server
Step 4 · Pipes & flow

Pipes are traced, flow arrows found

Process and signal lines are traced and joined on a 0.5 mm grid at every corner and branch, each with its own dash pattern and thickness. Flow arrows are found along the pipes.

Process linesSignal linesFlow arrows0.5 mm grid
Step 5 · Export

One DXF, every object on its own layer

Symbols go in as blocks, with pipes, flow arrows, texts and the sheet template each on their own layer. Open it in AutoCAD and switch the layers on and off.
