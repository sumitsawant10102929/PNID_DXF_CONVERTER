Put the drawing on a TCE sheet

The drawing's own border, title block and notes are taken off; the drawing is scaled to fit the template's drawing area (pipes stay on the grid), its notes go into the NOTES box, and the fields below go into the title block. They were read from the drawing's title block (): check them and correct anything wrong. In AutoCAD they can still be changed by double-clicking the title block.

What was found

Green: symbols · orange: OPC · red: equipment · blue: pipes · magenta triangles: flow arrows found. Click to open full size.

Layers in the DXF

Four groups (a tree in AutoCAD / TrueView's Layer Properties and in the DXF viewer):
SYMBOL: every symbol on one layer (each class in its own colour) · EQUIPMENT · PIPES: PIPES-Process lines, PIPES-Signal lines · FLOW ARROWS: the drawing's flow arrows as found · OTHERS: OTHERS-Text, -Line numbers, -Details, -Sheet template (border, title block, revision table, logo and their text), -Revision clouds, -Filled marks, -Notes, and on a TCE sheet the template's own layers. Only layers that have something on them are written.
